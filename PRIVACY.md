# Privacy

## The website

The website at https://deovandski.github.io/REPO-NAME/ has no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Everything on it runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) sees your IP address when it sends you the page.

The Settings menu (the gear, top right) saves your Language and Text size choices in your browser's local storage, under the key `REPO-NAME-site`, so they are remembered next time. They never leave your device; clearing the site's data removes them.

**Offline copies.** So the site (and the app, if you install it) opens without a connection, a service worker saves copies of the home page and of every page you open, with their styles, scripts and fonts, in your browser's Cache Storage. Images and anything from other websites are never saved. The copies stay on your device and are never sent anywhere; clearing the site's data removes them.

The website is also offered in other languages. Those versions were translated ahead of time by AI (Claude) from the English original and say so in a banner at the top.
